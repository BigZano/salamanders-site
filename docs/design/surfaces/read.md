# Read: /history

## Scope and mode
- Mode: Read. The visitor understands something; structure serves comprehension.
- Primary `src/views/History.vue` (/history, Version History). No related targets.

## Audience and job
- The forge and Legion leadership. The server decides who sees it; a refusal reads "Version History is restricted to the forge and Legion leadership", and a signed-out visitor gets only a sign-in prompt. Job: see what changed, when, and by whom. "Nothing here is ever removed."

## Action or task
- Filter (All, Perks, Builds, Privileges), search, open an entry for its note and before/after text, page back with "Older". Revert and revoke show only to those allowed.
- The page also holds a "Revoked privileges" section with "Reinstate", shown only to those who can revoke, and only when there are revocations.

## Proof and content
- The text is the content: dated entries, before/after descriptions, event snapshots. Nothing is summarised or invented.
- Entries carry member names at run time from the API. They never appear in the repo, a fixture or a screenshot.

## Constraints
- The page walls on Discord sign-in first; a refusal has short plain copy.
- Phone first: a left-aligned reading column, ledes near 52 characters, 44px taps, no horizontal page scroll (an expanded entry's raw snapshot wraps inside its panel).
- A long list that wants a quiet volcano profile (DESIGN.md, Layout) and has none today: /history falls to the catch-all profile. Text on dense panels at 4.5:1.

## Direction and memorable moment
- Memorable moment: none. Motion is feedback and state only (an entry opening, press, loading).
- Refuses: stat tiles, charts or a card grid over what is a dated record, and any glow or flame on a log. Heading, intro, controls, list: structure does the work.
