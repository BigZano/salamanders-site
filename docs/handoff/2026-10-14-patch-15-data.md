# Patch 15.0 data refresh: handoff

Branch `feat/patch-15-data` (worktree `.claude/worktrees/patch-15-data`), 2026-10-14. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-patch-15-data-refresh-design.md`. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-14-patch-15-data-refresh.md`.

Not merged and not pushed. No server, schema or Docker change. Nothing here needs a Vulkan deploy by itself.

## What shipped

- **Defence type per melee version** (Block, Balanced or Fencing) parsed from the wiki's `Defence` column into `weapon-trees.json`, and shown as a badge and in the version dropdown in the Armoury. The wiki's "Balance" is shown as "Balanced".
- **One-Handed Thunder Hammer** added (10 versions, Standard to Heroic "Power Maul"), plus the **Frost Axe** (Power Axe) and **Iron Hands** (Auto Bolt Rifle) heroics. The original Thunder Hammer is labelled "Two-Handed Thunder Hammer" in the UI; its key and saved builds are unchanged.
- **Class melee lists**: Tactical gains Power Sword, Power Axe and One-Handed Thunder Hammer; Assault and Bulwark gain One-Handed Thunder Hammer; Techmarine gains Thunder Hammer and One-Handed Thunder Hammer.
- **Tactical rework**: 11 grid slots changed, the prestige perk *Exigency Charge* became *Passive Chambering*, the starting-perk text was updated, and four wiki edits were held at their pre-15.0 text (see below). Every override lives in `src/data/patch-overrides.json` with its sources and warns when the wiki catches up.
- **Saved Tactical builds migrate on load** (perk builder, share links, local storage). Replaced perks are dropped along with their notes; reworked ones are kept and flagged. The builder shows a "Patch 15.0 rework" notice and gallery cards show a "Pre-15.0 · needs review" chip. Nothing is stored in the database.
- **Two opt-in bake flags** so the data can be refreshed without adopting every uncorroborated wiki edit: `wiki:perks -- --classes Tactical` and `wiki:weapons -- --keep-perks`.
- `last-patch.json` now records **Hotfix 15.1 (8 October)**.

## Adopting held data

- A class: `bun run wiki:perks -- --classes Heavy` (comma-separated for several). The other classes stay exactly as they are, including Tactical, because the previous bake is read from `perk-details.json` and `classes.json`. Overrides are applied on every run.
- All weapon drift (perks and stats together): `bun run wiki:weapons`. There is no per-weapon switch; to take one weapon's perk edit, use the perk-corrections editor.
- Anything you confirm is wrong on the wiki goes into `src/data/patch-overrides.json` as a pin, with its sources.

## Deploy and the weekly workflow

- The API reads the bake from `main` on GitHub with a 10-minute cache (`server/src/bake.js`), so once this is merged to `main` no redeploy is needed for the data.
- **The weekly `data-check` workflow runs `wiki:all` with no flags, so its PR will contain all of the held drift above.** Review it against this list before merging; do not merge it blindly. Overrides still apply on that run, so the Tactical corrections and pins survive it. If you would rather it never adopts held data automatically, change the workflow's commands to `--classes Tactical` and `--keep-perks` (a one-line edit, not done here).
